Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to Be Honest ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by E2 SOFT. We respect your privacy and are deeply committed to protecting any information that may be collected through our application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Be Honest is designed to be fully enjoyable without compromising personal identity:

  • We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal details like your name, address, or phone number.
  • We do not track your location or access your camera, contacts, or photo library.
  • Your responses, conversation answers, and quiz selections remain private on your device.

2. Information Collected for App Functionality

To provide core application services and in-app subscription management, we utilize third-party services:

  • In-App Purchases & Subscriptions: Payment transactions are handled directly by Apple through the App Store. We never see, store, or have access to your credit card or billing details.
  • Anonymous Identifiers: We utilize RevenueCat to securely validate active subscriptions and restore purchases. RevenueCat generates a randomized, anonymous user identifier (e.g., $RCAnonymousID) that is not linked to your personal identity and is never used for advertising tracking.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

Our app interacts with trusted service providers strictly to support essential functionality:

  • Apple In-App Purchases: Apple Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4. Children’s Privacy

Be Honest is intended for general mature audiences and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
